# Seenary 0.2.5 Beta

Released: 2026-10-19.

Tag: `v0.2.5-beta`

## Personal and Discover layouts

Arrange Personal and Discover to suit you. Give sections custom sizes, reorder or hide them, and remove and re-add them whenever you like. Seenary remembers your layout.

## Recommendations

### Since You Liked

Anime and manga suggestions are based on your favorites. Each suggestion offers Add to Library, Like, Dislike, and Not Interested.

- Adding a suggestion to your library keeps it in Since You Liked while you watch or read.
- After marking it Completed, you have 24 hours to react. A reaction replaces the suggestion with another available title.
- If you do not react within that time, Seenary adds a like the next time your recommendations update.
- Marking a suggested title Dropped treats it as Not Interested, without adding a dislike.
- Not Interested hides a title from your own Since You Liked section. You can undo it, and the title remains available elsewhere.
- Settings > Recommendation preferences keeps lists of Not Interested titles and disliked suggestions. Restore a hidden title or remove a dislike whenever you change your mind. Dislikes show the favorite and suggestion together. Restoring a title does not change a Dropped library status.
- Likes and dislikes help improve recommendations for everyone without changing your library favorites or scores.

### Seenary users recommend

Title details include suggestions that have received at least one like from Seenary users, ordered by recommendation count. Each title shows its recommendation and dislike counts. Four titles appear initially; Show all reveals more, up to 24 titles, and Show less collapses the list.

This section follows your preferred title language and adult-content filter. It stays hidden when there is nothing to display.

## Desktop release notifications

Receive notifications for titles marked Watching, Reading, or Planned in your library. Seenary must be running.

New episodes, Starts airing, Finishes airing, and Manga publications each have their own switch and timing choices. Select any combination of:

- 1 day before
- 12 hours before
- 6 hours before
- 1 hour before
- 30 minutes before
- 15 minutes before
- At release time

The default is 1 hour before and at release time. One main switch turns all release notifications off.

Anime reminders use the episode schedule. Premieres use Starts airing messages, and known finales use Final episode or Just finished airing messages instead of ordinary episode notifications.

Manga reminders count down to the start of the known publication date in your local time. They do not indicate an exact chapter release time.

Notifications use your preferred title language and appear above other desktop apps without taking focus. They fade in and out, stay for 10 seconds, and show the remaining time around the close button. Close them with the X or swipe sideways; short drags bounce back.

## Passwords and account recovery

Manage your Seenary password and recovery email under Settings > Sign-in methods.

Accounts created through AniList or MyAnimeList can set a Seenary password without already having an email. A password is required before adding a recovery email. Confirm the address with a six-digit code in the same window; codes expire after 10 minutes, work once, and can be resent.

Forgot password on the login screen sends a reset link to your verified recovery email. The link expires after 30 minutes, works once, and signs out existing sessions when you reset the password. A recovery email must be verified before it can help you recover access.

Unlinking an external account asks for your Seenary password. If you have not set one, you can create it as part of unlinking. Account deletion also requires a Seenary password, with password setup available there when needed.

You can cancel AniList or MyAnimeList sign-in while waiting for authorization.

## Find your next anime or manga

Combine genres and tags with publication filters, then select Browse. Genres and tags each default to All and have their own Must include all or Must include at least one rule. The two sections work together: a title must match both sections when both have selections.

Anime offers year and season filters. Manga offers publication year and publishing status, including Publishing, Finished publishing, Not yet published, On hiatus, and Cancelled. Year filters default to All years.

Genre and tag lists stay on one row until expanded, and each has a search field. Searching the choices keeps your selections. Both lists and Browse results follow your 18+ content setting.

## Search

Search now supports searching by genre or tag. Matching searches offer Genre only or Tag only choices, with dedicated results below the other search sections, grouped into Anime and Manga.

Title, genre, and tag results can still appear when character or studio searches are slow or unavailable. Saved searches remain available while refreshing.

## Browsing and settings

- The release calendar fills in as results arrive, so you can browse without waiting for everything to load. A result count shows progress, and loaded entries remain visible if more results cannot be retrieved.
- Related titles and Suggested watch order use consistent rows with library controls. Book and play icons distinguish manga from anime.
- Settings summary pills show the relevant choices in the same order as the controls. App information and desktop shortcut controls have their own sections.

## Fixes

- New AniList and MyAnimeList accounts automatically fetch their anime and manga libraries, even when ongoing automatic sync is off. Later sign-ins do not repeat the initial import.
- Skipping or completing the tutorial is remembered across restarts and sign-ins. You can replay it from Settings.
- Manga imported from AniList opens correctly from library cards and the entry editor. Manga available only through MyAnimeList keeps its correct identity.
- Library release-status badges update when you open fresh title details.
- Account deletion works safely while an external library import is running.
- Active sign-ins stay connected during use. Expired or revoked sign-ins return you to the login screen.
- Your choice about anonymous usage statistics is remembered instead of prompting again after sign-in.
- Settings section icons stay aligned with their headers while scrolling.
